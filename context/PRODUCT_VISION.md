# PRODUCT VISION — YOU

**Status: Active vision document. Not a claim of shipped functionality.**

## What YOU is

YOU is an open-source AI workspace: one browser product where a person configures their own AI providers (bring-your-own-key), picks models, and gets real work done — chat, research, artifacts, delegated agent work, automations — with premium interaction design and no artificial feature paywalls in the open-source core.

## Product shape

- **Product A — Marketing site:** the public front door. Explains the product, philosophy, providers, and open-source stance. Clear call to action.
- **Product B — Workspace (the app):** authenticated web application. Progressive navigation: **Chat · Models · Agents · Work · Research · Automations · Knowledge · Skills · Integrations · Activity · Settings** — most-used actions first, advanced controls contextual, never a wall of tabs.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **Open-source first** — Apache-2.0 core, inspectable, forkable, self-hostable.
2. **No fake features** — every visible control does something real. No simulated results, ever.
3. **User control** — users own their keys, data, memory, agent permissions, budgets, and exports.
4. **BYOK honesty** — open source does not make model APIs free; show real provider costs and spending controls.
5. **Privacy & isolation** — per-user data isolation; credentials encrypted at rest; secrets never in logs or client code.
6. **Browser-first** — open site → sign in → configure provider → select model → work. Self-host for technical users.

## Capability benchmarks (targets, not claims)

Conversational excellence (ChatGPT-class) · workspace/artifact orientation (Claude-class) · multimodal + productivity (Gemini-class) · deep research with citations · multi-agent delegation with durable state · scheduled automations · persistent permission-aware memory · reusable skills · one-person business operating system.

## Competitive stance

Benchmarks are behavioral targets. We never copy proprietary code, branding, or visual assets. Reference open-source projects are studied for patterns under their licenses (see docs/research/ and RESEARCH.md).
